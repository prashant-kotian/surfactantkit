The two agree to ~2–3%. This is a well-behaved, essentially linear van't Hoff relation. CONSISTENT.

### S-003
This blend is SDS (anionic) + DTAB (cationic) — a **catanionic** mixture, the textbook case of extreme non-ideality. Oppositely charged headgroups attract strongly (regular-solution β_M typically ≈ −10 to −20), producing enormous synergism and often precipitation/vesicle formation. Applying the ideal model to this system does not yield a physically meaningful CMC.
