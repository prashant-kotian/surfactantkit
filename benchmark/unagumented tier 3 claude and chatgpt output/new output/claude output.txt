### S-003
Mechanically: basis 100 g, SDS 60/288.38 = 0.20806 mol, DTAB 40/308.34 = 0.12973 mol; α_SDS = 0.6159, α_DTAB = 0.3841. 1/CMC = 0.6159/8.2 + 0.3841/15.0 = 0.07512 + 0.02560 = 0.10072 → CMC_mix = 9.93 mM. **Important physical caveat:** SDS (anionic) + DTAB (cationic) is a catanionic pair. Such oppositely-charged mixtures show strong electrostatic synergism (large negative β, β-values around −10 to −20 are typical), and the real mixed CMC is far below the ideal value — often by more than an order of magnitude — frequently accompanied by precipitation of the 1:1 catanionic salt. Clint's ideal equation assumes β = 0 and therefore does **not** validly describe this blend; the 9.93 mM figure is only the formal ideal-model output, not a realistic CMC.
