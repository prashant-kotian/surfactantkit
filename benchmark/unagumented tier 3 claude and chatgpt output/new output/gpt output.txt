For micellization, taking the micellization equilibrium constant as inversely proportional to the CMC gives

ln(CMC) = (Delta H_mic/R)(1/T) + constant.

Thus, for a temperature pair (T1, T2),

Delta H_mic = R ln(CMC2/CMC1)/(1/T2 - 1/T1).

The use of mM rather than another concentration unit does not affect this two-point result because only a concentration ratio appears. Convert the temperatures to kelvin: 303.15, 323.15, and 343.15 K.

For 30.0-50.0 C,

Delta H_mic = (8.3144626 J mol^-1 K^-1)ln(10.2/7.8)/(1/323.15 - 1/303.15)
            = -10.9252 kJ/mol.

For 50.0-70.0 C,

Delta H_mic = (8.3144626 J mol^-1 K^-1)ln(13.0/10.2)/(1/343.15 - 1/323.15)
            = -11.1819 kJ/mol.

The estimates differ by only 0.257 kJ/mol (about 2.3% of their magnitude), so they are reasonably CONSISTENT. The negative sign follows from the CMC increasing with temperature over this interval under the stated convention.
